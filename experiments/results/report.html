5. Experiment Methodology & Limitations

  • Models Evaluated: opencode/big-pickle and opencode/deepseek-v4.1-flash-free. Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite excluded due to absence of credentials in this environment.
  • Protocol: Verified clean isolated Git worktrees per execution with closed stdin handles and ephemeral XDG directories to eliminate child process deadlocks and SQLite DB contention.
  • Repeats: Repeat 0 complete across all 8 tasks (48/48 arms). Repeat 1 captured 19 valid executions before OpenCode free provider usage limits were reached.
  • Primary Metric: Realizable routers are evaluated on tokens per passed task under leave-one-task-out cross-validation. Fixed baseline achieves 100% pass rate at 50,814 tokens/passed task.
